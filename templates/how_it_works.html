GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a ZEQORO account to book?

No. Choose a meeting, select a time and enter your details. The host manages the schedule.

What if my timezone is different from the host's?

Available times are shown in the host's timezone, and the booking interface also displays your local time when your device is in a different timezone.

Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?

Yes. Use the private management link from your confirmation page. Keep that link private, since it gives access to your booking.

Does ZEQORO automatically sync with Google Calendar?

Yes—when the host connects Google Calendar through Workspace → Integrations. ZEQORO checks the host’s busy times and creates a Google event and Meet link. If Google is unavailable, the booking stays saved and sync can be retried. Hosts without a connection can still use downloadable .ics invitations.
